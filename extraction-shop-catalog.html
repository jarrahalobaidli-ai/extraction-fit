Shipping & Refund Policy

Where Extraction ships today, how long it takes, and your right to return — set to match Kuwait's consumer protection rules.

  • Ships to: GCC (Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman), Jordan, and Türkiye only — no other destinations right now.
  • GCC delivery: 4–7 business days from order confirmation.
  • Jordan & Türkiye delivery: Transit time confirmed with your order — ask Ops Freq for the current estimate.
  • Returns & refunds: 14 days from the date you receive your order, per Kuwait's Consumer Protection Law (No. 39/2014) and Digital Commerce Law (Decree-Law No. 10/2026) — full refund to your original payment method, no restocking fee.
  • Condition: Unused, unopened, in original packaging. Opened Grooming items (Beard Oil, Beard Balm, Beard Wash, Body Wash) can't be returned once opened.
  • Final sale: The 90-Day Extraction Protocol manual — once delivered as a digital download, it's non-refundable.
