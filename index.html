02 / SITUATION

You're not broken. You're undisciplined by design. Comfort did that — not weakness. Somewhere between the job, the routine, and the noise, the standard slipped, and no one called it.

I flew F/A-18s defending Kuwait's borders — precision, aggression, distance. Then I moved to search-and-rescue helicopters, and had to start from zero. New aircraft, new skills, new purpose. The hardest shift of my career wasn't technical — it was mental. From defending life at a distance to pulling it out of danger with my own hands. From shooting to saving.

Somewhere in that transition, "Spartan" — the callsign they gave me for the fight — stopped fitting. What replaced it was Gri Kurt: the grey wolf, older, tested, leading instead of striking first.

Extraction is that same restart, handed to you. Not every extraction happens on a battlefield. Discipline equals freedom.

03 / MISSION

Every operation starts with a distress call — Phase 0: MAYDAY, your intake and readiness screening. From there, four phases across thirteen weeks. Each one closes off the exit behind you before the next begins.

WEEKS 01–03

Designate Your Heading

Find your real starting point. Movement quality, foundational strength, and a full habit audit — accurate data, not aspiration.

WEEKS 04–07

Golden Hour

Progressive overload begins. Structural strength and work capacity build while the heaviest habit-formation work locks in.

WEEKS 08–11

Hold the Line

The critical window where intervention determines outcome. Peak intensity, hardest mental toughness work, nutrition dialed to goal.

WEEKS 12–13

RTB

Return to Base. Deload, retest benchmarks, lock in the data — and build the sustainment plan most programs never give you.
